Privacy Policy: Personal Assistant

Last updated 29 September 2026

"Personal Assistant" is a private, single-user tool built and run by Jason Camp for his own use. It is not offered to the public, and no one other than its owner can sign in to it.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, it reads and edits the owner's own Google Calendar (listing, creating, moving and deleting events) so it can answer questions about his schedule and send him reminders.

Where the data goes

  • Calendar data is processed on the owner's own home server and is not sold, shared or sent to any third party.
  • The sign-in token is stored only on that server, readable only by the owner's account.
  • Nothing is used for advertising, analytics or model training.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time from Google Account → Third-party connections.
